About St. John Paul Mugwanya Complex

Education for Quality Life

St. John Paul Mugwanya Complex Day and Boarding Primary School is a school in Masaka District, Uganda, that was started by local volunteers and teachers to give vulnerable, orphaned and disadvantaged children a chance to learn. Today it serves hundreds of pupils as both a day and a boarding school.

This page tells the story of the school, what it stands for, where it is, and what life is like for the children who learn here.

Our story

The school was founded by local volunteers and teachers who saw children in their own community who needed support: children who had lost parents, children who were vulnerable, and children from disadvantaged homes. They believed that every child, whatever their background, deserves the chance to learn, to grow and to build a good future.

That purpose still guides the school. It works as both a day school and a boarding school and serves hundreds of pupils, from the youngest children in Nursery to the Primary Seven class that sits the Primary Leaving Examination. It remains committed to care for every child, an inclusive classroom and good teaching.

Our mission and vision

Mission

To deliver a high-quality, inclusive primary education that inspires a lifelong love of learning and critical thinking.

Vision

To be a leading primary school known for academic excellence, dedicated teachers, and well-rounded students.

What guides us: head, heart and hand

We want to train our children to become self-reliant leaders in their communities. We give them a conducive environment in which to pursue excellence, in both academic and practical skills, where the rights and needs of children are met and protected. Our approach rests on three ideas.

Head

We train the minds of our children by providing formal education, from the first reading lessons to the Primary Leaving Examination.

Heart

We train the hearts of our children and build a moral bank in the hearts of learners, so that they grow in honesty, respect and kindness.

Hand

We train the body of each child through co-curricular activities such as music, dance and drama, sports, creative modelling, weaving and agriculture.

Who we serve and how we care

  • Children from the local community, including those who are orphaned, vulnerable or from disadvantaged backgrounds, were the reason the school was founded.
  • Day scholars attend school each day and go home in the evening. Boarding scholars live at school, where their accommodation, meals and supervision are provided.
  • Teachers are dedicated to helping every child to learn, whatever his or her starting point.
  • The rights and needs of children are met and protected, in the classroom, in the dormitory and on the playground.

Life beyond the classroom

Learning at Mugwanya Complex does not stop when the lesson ends. Pupils can join clubs and movements that build skills, character and friendships. Each one has its own page that explains what members do.

  • Red Cross
  • Scouting
  • Girl Guides
  • Debating Club
  • School Choir
  • Drama Club
  • Scripture Union
  • Wildlife Club
