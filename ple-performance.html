PLE Performance

Our results in the Primary Leaving Examination over the last 20 years.

The Primary Leaving Examination (PLE) is the national examination taken at the end of Primary Seven. It is set and marked by the Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB). It tests four subjects: English, Mathematics, Science and Social Studies. A pupil’s results are summed up as a division, from Division 1 (the best) down to Division 4, or Ungraded.

The table below shows, for each year from 2006 to 2025, how many of our candidates passed in the first, second and third grades (Divisions 1, 2 and 3), together with the percentage of all candidates that this number represents.

How to read the table

  • Candidates is the total number of Primary Seven pupils of our school who sat the PLE that year.
  • No. is the number of candidates who obtained that division.
  • % is the share of all candidates that this number represents. It is worked out as the number in the division, divided by the total candidates, multiplied by 100.

Example: if 40 candidates sat the examination and 12 obtained Division 1, then the Division 1 percentage is 12 ÷ 40 × 100 = 30%.

How divisions are decided

In each of the four subjects a pupil receives a grade from 1 (best) to 9. The four grades are added together to give an aggregate, which can be as low as 4 and as high as 36. The lower the aggregate, the better the result. The aggregate decides the division:

Division 1 (first grade)4 to 12
Division 2 (second grade)13 to 23
Division 3 (third grade)24 to 29
