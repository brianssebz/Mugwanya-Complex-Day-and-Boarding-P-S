Our curriculum

What your child learns, and how it leads to the Primary Leaving Examinations.

We teach the Ugandan national curriculum, and learners sit the Primary Leaving Examinations (P.L.E.) in Primary Seven.

Our Pre-Primary to Primary Seven programmes focus on student outcomes. They are built from integrated units that cover each of the key learning areas in Ugandan primary schools.

These units are part of a continuous cycle of review, so the programme keeps pace with the needs of our learners and the priorities of each school department.

Primary Leaving Examinations

The national examination taken at the end of Primary Seven. Preparation begins in Pre-Primary, not in the final year.

  • English
  • Mathematics
  • Science
  • Social Studies

A learning journey in four stages

Each stage builds on the one before it, so learners move on with confidence.

  1. Pre-Primary

    Baby, Middle and Top class

    Learning through play, songs and stories. Children build early reading, counting, speaking and social skills in a safe, caring setting.

  2. Lower Primary

    Primary One to Three

    Integrated themes link what children learn to daily life. The focus is on strong reading, writing and number foundations.

  3. Upper Primary

    Primary Four to Six

    Subject teaching deepens in English, Mathematics, Science and Social Studies. Learners do projects, research and take on leadership roles.

  4. Primary Seven

    P.L.E. year

    Focused revision, practice papers and guidance, so every learner is ready for the Primary Leaving Examinations and the next school.

The key learning areas

Five learning areas make up the core programme at St. John Paul Mugwanya Complex.

English

A comprehensive literacy programme, supported by extensive resources, that builds the language skills learners need for the future.

  • Reading, phonics and comprehension
  • Grammar, handwriting and composition
  • Speaking, storytelling and debate

Mathematics

A step-by-step numeracy programme from Pre-Primary to Primary Seven that develops mathematical competence and confidence.

  • Number work and the four operations
  • Measurement, geometry and data
  • Problem solving with real-life examples

Science and Social Studies

Effective programmes that give children the skills and knowledge to discover the world around them safely and productively.

  • Observation, experiments and nature study
  • Our community, Uganda and map work
  • Caring for the environment and staying safe

Health and Physical Education

Every child takes part in Physical Education, building sporting skills and lifelong healthy habits.

  • Games, athletics and team sports
  • Fitness, hygiene and nutrition
  • Teamwork, fair play and discipline

The Arts

Every class programme includes artistic activities expressed and shared through dance, drama, media and visual arts.

  • Drawing, painting and craft
  • Music, dance and drama performances
  • Creative thinking and self-expression

School discipline: the 3Hs

Good results and good character grow together.

We train our children to become self-reliant leaders in their communities. We give them a conducive environment in which to pursue excellence, in both academic and practical skills, where the rights and needs of every child are met and protected.

Basic discipline in our school is imparted through three domains, the 3Hs.

Head

The mind

We train the minds of our children by providing formal education.

Heart

Character and values

We train the hearts of our children and build a moral bank in the hearts of learners.

Hand

Body and practical skills

We train the body of the children we care for through co-curricular activities: music, dance and drama, sports, creative modelling, weaving and agricultural principles.

Co-curricular activities

Music

Tracking progress and supporting learners

Parents should always know how their child is doing, and what comes next.

How we assess

  • Continuous assessment. Class work, exercises and short tests throughout the term.
  • End-of-term examinations. Results are shared with parents in a termly report.
  • Primary Seven mock exams. Practice under P.L.E. conditions before the real examination.

How we support

  • Caring teachers. Class teachers know each child and follow their progress.
  • Revision and extra help. Learners who need more time get extra support.
  • Parent partnership. We work with families so school and home pull in the same direction.
