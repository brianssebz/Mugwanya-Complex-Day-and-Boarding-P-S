Our Services

Our Services

Learning, care and activities for every child.

St. John Paul Mugwanya Complex offers a complete primary education from Nursery to Primary Seven, for both day and boarding pupils. Here is what the school provides: the curriculum, the learning areas, care for each child’s character, and activities beyond the classroom.

Curriculum

Curriculum
The Ugandan national curriculum, with the Primary Leaving Examination (P.L.E.) in Primary Seven
Classes
Nursery, Primary One to Primary Seven
Learner types
Day scholars and boarding scholars

Core curriculum programmes

From Pre-Primary to Primary Seven, our programmes focus on student outcomes. They are built from integrated units that cover each of the key learning areas in Ugandan primary schools. These units are part of a continuous cycle of review, so that they keep meeting the needs of our learners and the priorities of each school department.

The key learning areas

English

A comprehensive literacy programme, enhanced with extensive resources, that builds the language skills learners will need in the future.

  • Reading, phonics and comprehension
  • Grammar, handwriting and composition
  • Speaking, storytelling and debate

Mathematics

A sequential numeracy programme from Pre-Primary to Primary Seven, aimed at developing mathematical competence among the pupils.

  • Number work and the four operations
  • Measurement, geometry and data
  • Problem solving with real-life examples

Science and Social Studies

Effective programmes that give children the skills and knowledge to become intrinsically motivated to discover the world in a safe and productive way.

  • Observation and simple experiments
  • Our community, Uganda and map work
  • Caring for the environment and staying safe

Health and Physical Education

All children have the opportunity to take part in Physical Education programmes that develop specific sporting skills and life-long health habits.

  • Games, athletics and team sports
  • Fitness and personal hygiene
  • Teamwork and fair play

The Arts

All class programmes include artistic pursuits expressed and communicated through dance, drama, media and visual arts.

  • Drawing, painting and craft
  • Music, dance and drama
  • Creative thinking and self-expression

School discipline

We train our children to become self-reliant leaders in their communities by providing a conducive environment in which to pursue excellence in both academic and practical skills, where the rights and needs of children are met and protected. Basic discipline is imparted through three domains, the 3Hs.

Head

We train the minds of our children by providing formal education.

Heart

We train the hearts of our children and build a moral bank in the hearts of learners.

Hand

We train the body of the children we care for by promoting co-curricular activities: music, dance and drama, sports, creative modelling, weaving and agricultural principles.

Day and boarding services

Day scholars

Pupils who study at school and return home each day.

  • Lunch is included for day scholars.
  • School uniform is bought separately from the school office.
  • Fees are paid per term, at the beginning of each term.

Boarding scholars

Pupils who live at school during the term.

  • Boarding fees include accommodation, meals and supervision.
  • Boarders bring a bedding set (sheets, pillow and blanket), a towel and personal toiletries, as well as the usual school items.
  • The full list for each class is in the school circular.

School uniform

All uniforms must be purchased directly from the school administration at the designated times. The costs depend on sizes and are available from the bursar’s office.

Clubs and movements

Our co-curricular programme gives pupils a chance to discover their talents and to serve others. Choose a club to read what its members do.

  • Red Cross
  • Scouting
  • Girl Guides
  • Debating Club
  • School Choir
  • Drama Club
  • Scripture Union
  • Wildlife Club

Admissions and support

Parents and guardians who would like to enrol a child can apply online or contact the school office, which can explain the fees, the requirements for each class and the steps to follow.
